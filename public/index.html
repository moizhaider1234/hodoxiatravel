Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book.

Where do you operate?

Hodoxia Travel is based in Canada and operates only within Canada. Choose from the cities listed on our coverage page when you book.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Airport and event dates fill up first. For short-notice trips, send a request and we will tell you what is possible.

Do you monitor my flight?

Yes. Add your flight number in the notes when you book and your chauffeur will track it for delays and early arrivals.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Change and cancellation terms are confirmed with your booking. Contact us as soon as your plans change and we will help.

Which vehicle should I choose?

Business class suits most solo and two-person trips. First class adds flagship comfort. Business SUVs suit groups of up to six and extra luggage.

How do I pay?

Your fare is quoted in Canadian dollars and confirmed before your trip. We will share payment options when we confirm your booking.
