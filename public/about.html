About HODOXIA

A Canadian chauffeur company built around one idea: the journey should be as considered as the destination.

Who We Are

Refined chauffeur-driven transportation

HODOXIA Travel is dedicated to delivering refined chauffeur-driven transportation with an emphasis on comfort, professionalism and dependable service across Canada.

We believe a ride should give you room to think, rest or work. That means a professional chauffeur who knows the road, a spotless vehicle that is ready when you are, and a fare you have confirmed before you travel.

HODOXIA Travel Inc. is a Canadian company. Our service is built for travellers who value time and calm: executives between meetings, families arriving home, and guests celebrating a milestone.

What drives us

Four principles guide how we plan every journey and how we treat every guest.

  1. 01

    Comfort

    A quiet cabin, generous rear space and a smooth, direct ride. Travel should leave you rested, not drained.

  2. 02

    Professionalism

    Courteous, well-presented chauffeurs who arrive early, communicate clearly and handle the details without being asked.

  3. 03

    Dependability

    We plan for traffic, follow your flight and confirm everything ahead of time, so the plan holds when your day changes.

  4. 04

    Discretion

    Your itinerary and conversations stay private. We are licensed, insured and quietly attentive.
