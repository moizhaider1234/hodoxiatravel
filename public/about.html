Who We Are

Refined chauffeur-driven transportation

HODOXIA Travel is dedicated to delivering refined chauffeur-driven transportation with an emphasis on comfort, professionalism and dependable service across Canada.

We exist for the traveller who wants the journey to feel as considered as the destination. Every ride is arranged around you: a professional chauffeur, a spotless vehicle and a fare confirmed before you travel, so you can arrive composed and on time.

HODOXIA Travel Inc. is a Canadian company. Our chauffeurs are licensed, insured and discreet, and our team is reachable at any hour for changes, questions and last-minute needs.

What we stand for

Three commitments shape every journey we take with you.

Comfort

Late-model business and first class sedans and SUVs, detailed before every trip. A quiet cabin, generous rear space and a calm, direct ride from pickup to drop-off.

Professionalism

Courteous, well-presented chauffeurs who know the roads and the airports. They arrive early, greet you properly and handle your luggage without being asked.

Dependable service

We follow your flight, plan for traffic and confirm every detail ahead of time. When you land early or late, your chauffeur is there.
